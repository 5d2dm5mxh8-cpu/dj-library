Genres & tags
Separate tags from genres
A genre stays a genre — that is what Mixxx's Genre column, the rekordbox import and your smart crates read. Tokens marked with “#”, and genres carried by only one or two tracks, are usually tags: move one across and nothing else about the track changes.
Auto-clean new downloads
When a song finishes downloading, high-confidence Spotify matches are written straight to its tags automatically — weaker matches stay here for you to review
Auto-tag genres
On new downloads, fill in a missing genre from the best available source (Discogs, then MusicBrainz, then Spotify). Existing genres are left alone.
Sweep genres after scans
After each library scan, run the background genre fill automatically for every track still missing a genre — so new downloads get tagged without pressing Start. Existing genres are left alone, and the sweep can be Canceled like any other.
Only scan tracks never scanned
Skips anything a previous cleanup already looked at — scans get faster and your manual tag edits are never overwritten later
